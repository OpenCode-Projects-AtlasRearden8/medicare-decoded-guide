# Worksheet: My Deadlines

One page for your whole enrollment calendar, from
[Enrollment Windows, Calmly](../02-enrollment-windows.md). Fill it once,
check it every fall, and stop letting television commercials define your
deadlines.

*Education and paperwork help only — never medical, legal, financial, or
plan advice.*
