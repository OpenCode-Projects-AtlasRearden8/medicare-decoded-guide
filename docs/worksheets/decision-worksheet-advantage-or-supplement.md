# Worksheet: Advantage or Supplement?

A fill-in worksheet for the [Advantage vs. Supplement chapter](../04-advantage-vs-supplement.md).
Print it, sit with it for twenty minutes, and let the answer be *yours* —
this sheet never picks for you; it collects what you already value and
turns it into questions for a free SHIP counselor.

*Education and paperwork help only — never medical, legal, financial, or
plan advice.*

## Step 1 — What I value (check all that feel true)

```
[ ] The lowest possible monthly bill matters most to me
[ ] I want costs to be predictable, even if the monthly price is higher
[ ] Keeping my current doctors matters more than anything else
[ ] I spend months a year away from home (snowbird / family / travel)
[ ] I would trade some provider choice for extras (dental, vision, gym)
[ ] I don't want pre-approvals and referral paperwork
[ ] I don't mind a rules-following plan if the premium is $0
[ ] A big surprise bill would hurt more than a steady monthly cost
```

## Step 3 — My round-number math

Fill with numbers from real plan papers (Summaries of Benefits), not ads.
Use the teaching-number habit from the guide: round to tens and hundreds.

## Step 4 — My questions for SHIP (free, unbiased, sells nothing)

```
1. "Here are my three doctors and five drugs - do these two plans
   actually cover them this year?"
2. "Which plan's worst case fits my budget better, given my health?"
3. "What enrollment window am I in right now, and is there any
   penalty risk in my timing?" (See the windows chapter.)
4. "Am I likely to qualify for Extra Help or an MSP?"
   (See the Extra Help chapter - the math changes.)
5. "What would you check before I sign anything?"
```
