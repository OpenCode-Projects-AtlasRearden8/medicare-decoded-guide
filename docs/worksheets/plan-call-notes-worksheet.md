# Worksheet: Plan Call Notes

A call log for every Medicare-related phone call, from
[Avoiding Plan-Switching Scams](../07-avoiding-scams.md) and the
[binder chapter](../08-enrollment-binder.md). Confirmation numbers are
the whole game: a logged call is a fact, an unlogged call is a memory.

*Education and paperwork help only — never medical, legal, financial, or
plan advice.*

## Before the call (one minute)

```
[ ] I dialed THEM (an official number), not the other way around
[ ] I know what I want from this call, in one sentence:
      ______________________________________________________
[ ] My card and papers are in front of me
[ ] I have a pen and this page open
```

## Phrases worth having in front of you

```
"What plan am I currently enrolled in, and when did it take effect?"
"Can you note my file with what you just told me, and give me a
 confirmation number?"
"I think I may qualify for a Special Enrollment Period.
 Can you check what windows are open for my situation?"
"Please mail me the details. I need to think about it."
 (That last one is for incoming calls you didn't ask for.)
```

## After the call (two minutes)

```
[ ] Wrote the confirmation number above
[ ] Filed the related letter in Tab 4 of the binder
[ ] If something was promised: noted WHO promised it and BY WHEN
[ ] If the call was a surprise sales call: logged it anyway -
      patterns matter if a report to SHIP or the state
      insurance department comes later
```

## The golden rule of every call

You may end any call, at any time, with:

> "Thank you, I have what I need for now. Goodbye."

Nobody legitimate will argue with it.

*Education and paperwork help only — never medical, legal, financial, or
plan advice.*
