# The Education-Only Promise

## What this guide is

The Medicare Decoded Guide is **education and paperwork help**. It
teaches you to read Medicare's letters, windows, plans, and programs so
that paperwork stops being scary and decisions become yours.

## What this guide is not

- **It is never plan advice.** It does not recommend Advantage over
  Original, Medigap over Advantage, one plan over another, or any
  company at all. Where it compares, it compares *categories* with
  clearly invented round numbers.
- **It is never medical advice.** It contains no treatment
  information, no symptom guidance, and no health instructions of any
  kind. Health questions belong to you and your health care team.
- **It is never legal or financial advice.** Tax rules, penalties, and
  eligibility decisions are made by the official offices — Social
  Security, 1-800-MEDICARE, your state Medicaid office — not by a
  reading guide.

## Where the real decisions happen

| Question type | Where you go |
| --- | --- |
| "What plan am I in? What changed?" | 1-800-MEDICARE |
| "What should I sign up for, given my situation?" | Social Security, and a free SHIP counselor |
| "Can I afford my drugs and premiums?" | Social Security (Extra Help), your state Medicaid office (MSPs) |
| "Does this plan cover my doctor and my drugs?" | The plan's own Summary of Benefits, formulary, and directories |
| "Is this call a scam?" | Hang up, then call 1-800-MEDICARE or SHIP yourself |
| Anything about your health | Your health care team |

## One sentence, capitalized once

**THIS GUIDE EXPLAINS PAPER. IT NEVER PICKS PLANS, NEVER ADVISES ABOUT
HEALTH, AND NEVER ASKS YOU FOR PERSONAL INFORMATION — BECAUSE IT CAN'T:
IT IS A BOOK.**

## How to use it safely

- Treat every number in this guide as a **teaching number** until you
  replace it with one from your own papers.
- Verify anything that matters with the official sources above —
  especially anything a deadline depends on.
- Keep your [binder](08-enrollment-binder.md) as the single place where
  decisions and confirmations live, so future-you can always retrace.

*Education and paperwork help only — never medical, legal, financial, or
plan advice.*
