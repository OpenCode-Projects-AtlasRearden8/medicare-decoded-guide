# The Medicare Decoded Guide

## Start Here

A folder of plan brochures arrived in the mail. Or a birthday is coming up.
Or someone said "you have to pick something by December 7th" and now the
whole fall feels like a deadline. Take a breath. You are going to be fine.

Medicare paperwork is a lot of **letters** (A, B, C, D), a lot of **windows**
(enrollment periods), and a lot of **fall mail**. It is not a test you can
fail. This manual translates all three into plain English, round numbers,
and one binder you control — explained like a friend would: step by step,
never condescending.

**This is education and paperwork help only.** Nothing here is medical,
legal, or financial advice — and it is never *plan* advice. This guide will
never tell you which plan to pick, which doctor to see, or what to do about
your health. It teaches you how to read the paperwork so you can make your
own decisions, and it consistently points you to the right official helpers:
**1-800-MEDICARE**, the free **SHIP** counselors in every state, and
**Social Security** for sign-ups.

## Who this is for

- Anyone approaching 65 (or helping someone who is) and facing the
  enrollment questions for the first time.
- Anyone drowning in the fall plan-mail pile and wondering what actually
  changed.
- Family helpers and caregivers who need the vocabulary, not the sales pitch.
- Every chapter uses **round, made-up teaching numbers** ($100 premiums,
  $500 copays) so the *idea* stays clear. Your real numbers come from your
  own plan papers, never from a guide.

## The short version

1. **Medicare is federal health insurance, mostly for people 65 and older.**
   Part A is hospital, Part B is doctors, Part D is drugs, and Part C is the
   private all-in-one way to get all of them.
2. **There are really only a few enrollment windows** — one around your
   65th birthday, one each fall, one each early spring, and special ones
   when life changes. Missing one usually costs more, but it is rarely a
   catastrophe.
3. **You will choose between two "doors":** Original Medicare (the
   government runs it, you add a supplement and a drug plan) or a Medicare
   Advantage plan (one private plan runs everything). Both are legal,
   normal, and reversible at specific times of year.
4. **Never decide from a TV ad or a phone call.** Compare from the plan's
   own papers, with round numbers, and confirm with a free SHIP counselor.
5. **Free help exists:** Extra Help for drug costs, Medicare Savings
   Programs for premiums, and unbiased SHIP counseling at no charge.
6. **Keep a binder.** Twenty papers a year stop being chaos when they have
   tabs.
